INSTRUCTIONS

Your discipline data stays on this device. No login, server, or cloud is required.

LOCAL DATA

Challenge progress, individual daily goals, completion states and records are stored in localStorage.

APP DATA

Everything persists after closing, reopening, leaving the page, or refreshing the browser.

NO CLOUD

This single-file app does not send your challenge data to a server.

ONE ACTIVE CHALLENGE

Only one challenge can be active at a time. Pack a completed challenge into Records before starting another.

DAILY GOALS

Every day can have its own goals. During setup you can add, edit, delete, copy to the next day, or copy to selected days.

DAY SYSTEM

Day 1 begins on the challenge start date. Only today's day can be completed. Future days stay locked. Uncompleted past days become MISSED.

RECORD SYSTEM

When every day is completed, PACK & SAVE RECORD stores the complete daily history. Record Details can export a visual PNG record with all daily goals.
